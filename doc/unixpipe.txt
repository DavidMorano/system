UNIXPIPE

	Notes:
	In the old days (or on many BSD type UNIX® systems to the
	present day, and on Linux) the two resulting pipe ends from
	|pipe(2)| were unidirectional as follows:

	end	access
	---------------
	0	reading
	1	writing

	With the advent of System V UNIX® (officiially in 1984 or
	earlier) both ends are bidirectional.
